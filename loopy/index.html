The Craft

Every stitch,
placed by hand

No machines, no moulds. Each piece starts with a hook, a ball of yarn, and a pattern traced from imagination. What emerges is something that carries the warmth of the hands that made it.

1
Choose & SketchThe idea starts as a drawing or a reference — a creature, a flower, a shape worth making real.
2
Select the YarnColour, weight, and texture are chosen for each piece individually. Soft acrylics for toys, structured cotton for décor.
3
Hook & StuffCrocheted in the round using the amigurumi technique — a tight, seamless fabric that holds its shape and stays safe for little hands.
4
Finish & InspectAny final embroidery added. Each piece is checked by hand before it goes out the door.

Behind the loops

Made with
care, not machine!

Loopy Thingies is a small handmade business built on the simple belief that objects made slowly, by hand, carry something that mass-produced things simply can't.

Every item is crocheted to order — which means you can ask for custom colours, sizes, or characters. If you can imagine it and it can be looped, we'll give it a go.

Reach out on Instagram or drop a message to place a custom order, ask about availability, or just say hello.
